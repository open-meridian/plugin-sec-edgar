# Terms

The plugin reads EDGAR, run by the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission.
This is a summary in our words, not legal advice: read SEC's own pages
before you rely on it.

## SEC's terms, in brief

- **Filings are public records.** What companies file with SEC is
  published for anyone to read and reuse.
- **Fair access.** SEC asks every automated client to declare a User-Agent
  with a name and contact address, and to send no more than 10 requests a
  second. Clients that do not are refused, for a time. The plugin keeps
  both: it asks nothing without a User-Agent, and paces itself at the rate
  you set, never above 10.
- **No endorsement.** SEC's name and seal may not be used to suggest it
  endorses a product. This plugin is not affiliated with, endorsed by or
  certified by SEC.

SEC's own pages on accessing EDGAR data and on its website's policies are on
[sec.gov](https://www.sec.gov).

## Your due diligence

Whether a use of filings suits your firm is yours to decide: your own
policies, and any terms of the systems that read the lake, still apply.
Record your decision in the dataset's licence on the **Data sources** page.
